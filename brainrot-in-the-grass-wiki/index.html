ASK A QUESTION ↗
What is Brainrot in the Grass?+

An independently developed Roblox game centered on searching tall grass for Brainrot encounters, carrying discoveries back to a base and building a collection.

How many rarity tiers are there?+

There are eight named tiers: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, Secret and Glitched. Exact encounter odds are not published on this wiki without verification.

What happens after I find a character?+

The core loop involves bringing your discovery back to your base and placing it on a pedestal. Other players may try to interrupt a carry along the way.

Where can I play the game?+

A verified public Roblox experience link has not yet been added to this site. The wiki will include one when the creator provides it.

Is this wiki complete?+

No. It's an actively maintained development guide. Individual rarity assignments, character images, spawn statistics and update details are only added after they're confirmed.
